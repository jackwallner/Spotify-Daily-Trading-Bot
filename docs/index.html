🤖 How This Works

💡 The Simple Version

This is a robot that makes predictions about whether Bitcoin will go up or down. Every 15 minutes, it looks at the market, asks an AI "should I make a prediction?", and either places a small prediction or waits for a better opportunity.

🎯 What's a Prediction Market?

It's a place where you can make predictions about the future. If your prediction is correct, you win money. If you're wrong, you lose what you put in.

🪙 Example:

Question: "Will Bitcoin go above $88,500 in the next hour?"

👉 You put 20 cents on "YES"

✅ If Bitcoin goes up → You get $1.00 (profit: 80 cents!)

❌ If Bitcoin stays down → You lose your 20 cents

🔄 What Happens Every 15 Minutes

1️⃣ Look at Bitcoin
Is the price going up? Down? Are lots of people buying or selling?
2️⃣ Give it a Score
0-100 score. High = price probably going up. Low = probably going down.
3️⃣ Ask the AI
"Hey Gemini, here's what I see. Should I predict or wait?"
4️⃣ Predict or Skip
If AI says predict → spends ~20 cents. If AI says skip → waits for better chance.
5️⃣ Wait for Result
One hour later: Did Bitcoin go up or down? Win or lose?

🎬 Example: A Smart Skip

🪙 The Question: "Will Bitcoin hit $88,500 in the next hour?"

📊 Bot checks the market:

Price is dropping ⬇️ ... Score: 38/100 (low = probably going down)

🤖 Bot asks AI:

"Should I predict that Bitcoin goes UP?"

💭 AI says:

"Nope! Score is low, price is dropping. SKIP. Wait for a better chance."

⏰ One hour later:

Bitcoin dropped even more! ❌

Good thing it skipped! Would have lost 20 cents. 💰

🧠 Does It Get Smarter?

Yes! The AI looks at past wins and losses to make better decisions.

📅 Day 1: Makes 10 predictions. Wins 6, loses 4.

🔍 Day 2: AI notices: "I lost when the market was crazy. Next time, I'll skip those."

📈 Day 3: Skips the risky ones. Wins more!

❓ Quick Questions

🎲 Is this just luck?
No! This uses math and AI to find good predictions. It's data-driven, not random.

💸 Can it lose money?
Yes, sometimes. Nobody can predict the future perfectly. The goal is to win more than you lose.

⏭️ Why does it skip sometimes?
When it's not sure, it waits. Skipping bad predictions is just as smart as making good ones!

💰 How much per prediction?
About 20 cents. Small amounts = small risk while it learns.
